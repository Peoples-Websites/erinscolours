Let Us See You – APS Gallery Edition

The Let Us See You project is a 12-month inclusive curatorial initiative by Aire Place Studios (APS) showcasing emerging talent from Yorkshire.
Event Overview
• Name: Let Us See You – APS Gallery Edition
• Organizer: Aire Place Studios
• Location: Leeds, UK
• Focus: Supporting underrepresented, disabled, neurodivergent, and emerging regional artists
Key Details & Dates
• First Edition: Ran from 23 February to 22 March 2024 at the APS Gallery.
• Second Edition: Ran from 14 June to 19 July 2024, featuring over 50 regional artists.
• Partner Showcases: Extended activities and displays have also partnered with venues like Left Bank Leeds.

"Serving the District" is a collaborative street art mural located at the Morrisons store in Idle, created as part of the BD: Walls talent development program for Bradford 2025 UK City of Culture.
Mural Overview
• Title: Serving the District
• Location: Morrisons Store, Idle, Bradford
• Partners: Commissioned through Bradford 2025 in partnership with the Morrisons Foundation and street art collective Graffwerk
Key Design Elements
• Heritage Tribute: Features a traditional market stall referencing William Morrison’s original egg and butter stall opened in Bradford's Rawson Market in 1899.
• Local Identity: Highlights fresh produce, livestock, and iconic landmarks including Bradford City Hall and Lister Mills.
• Community Focus: Incorporates symbols celebrating the district's South Asian community and the city's cultural legacy.

2025 Bradford Art Show

The Bradford Art Show 2025 took place from December 6 to December 21, 2025, at the Loading Bay pop-up venue on Duke Street in Bradford City Centre.

Event Details
• Dates & Times: December 6 – 21, 2025; open Thursday – Saturday from 12:00 PM to 6:00 PM, and Sundays from 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM.
• Location: Loading Bay, Duke Street, Bradford.
• Cost: Free admission.
Exhibition Overview
• Theme: "OUR PLACE," focusing on local identity, experiences, and creative voices across the district.
• Highlights: Featured 124 works by 105 local artists—ranging from painting and drawing to sculpture, ceramics, film, and textiles.
• Festival Context: Part of the inaugural OUR TURN visual arts festival, running alongside the Turner Prize 2025 hosted at Cartwright Hall Art Gallery.

https://bradfordculture.co.uk/event/our-turn-bradford-art-show/


2025 1. Hull Graff Jam (Kingston upon Hull, UK)

Bankside Gallery Hull hosted the annual Hull Graff Jam 2025.
• Location: Bankside Gallery, Hull, UK.
• Details: Organised by local groups and creators like Yorkshire Vandals Unite, the event brought together regional street artists and writers to paint legal wall spaces provided by the gallery.
• Media Coverage: Local photographer and filmmaker Elizabeth Carter Media documented the 2025 event via dedicated community video releases highlighting the local urban art scene.
